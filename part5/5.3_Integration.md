# Product & Integration

Following the previous question, I'll focus on making sure the AI is given the minimal premissions and data it needs to do it's job. That's why I'll focus on understaing the goal of integrating AI into the system. I would ask the Product Manager the following questions:

1. **Goal and success criteria:** Which problems should the AI help solve, and how will we measure success: reduced downtime, faster resolution, fewer technician visits, or another outcome? What rate of unnecessary or harmful recommendations is acceptable?

2. **Recommendation versus execution:** Will the AI only recommend actions, prepare them for approval, or trigger them automatically? Which actions, such as rebooting or disconnecting a station, are allowed, and which must always be prohibited?

3. **Permissions and approval:** Who can view, approve, and execute each action? Should disruptive actions require an explicit confirmation or a second approver, and who is accountable for the final decision?

4. **Operational impact:** Could an action interrupt an active session, affect customer safety, or disconnect other stations? Which conditions must block an action, and should maintenance windows, customer notifications, or alternative service availability be required?

5. **Evidence and uncertainty:** What telemetry, device history, and troubleshooting guidance must support a recommendation? How fresh and complete must that data be, and when should the AI abstain or escalate to a human instead of recommending an action?

6. **User experience:** What should the operator see before approving: the reason, supporting evidence, expected benefit, affected stations, downtime, risks, and alternatives? Can the operator reject the recommendation or provide feedback?

7. **Execution safeguards:** How should we handle stale recommendations, changed station conditions, duplicate requests, or conflicting operator actions? What cooldown periods and limits should prevent repeated reboots or too many stations being disconnected at once?

8. **Failure and recovery:** How will we confirm that an action succeeded and improved the issue? If the station loses connectivity or fails to recover, is remote recovery possible, who gets alerted, and when is an on-site technician required?
